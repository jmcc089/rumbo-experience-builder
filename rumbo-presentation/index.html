The Problem

Planning a trip is not a lookup.

It is temporal constraint satisfaction plus optimization. Feasibility is only half of it, because an itinerary also has to be good: paced right, matched to what the traveler wants, and priced against the marked-up total, never the provider's net.

Bounded day

Each day is a continuous window every activity has to fit inside.

Inter-zone transfers

Activities chain by feasible travel time across 13 zones.

Operating hours

An experience only runs while its provider is actually open.

Sunrise & tide

Some activities are sunrise-only or depend on the tide.

No early starts

Honored as a hard constraint when the traveler rules them out.

Pace & variety

The day has to breathe, and the trip must not repeat itself.

The Engine

Valid first, good second.

assemble() is the single entry point over one CSP and scoring core. Nothing that violates a hard constraint is ever scored, so feasibility and quality stay cleanly separable and each is testable on its own.

Layer 1 — Hard constraints

Binary. Transfers, hours, dependencies, budget. A candidate day either passes or it is discarded.

Layer 2 — Weighted scoring

Five metrics normalized 0 to 1, combined by a weight vector derived from the traveler's preferences.

The five metrics
transfer efficiency interest match pace breathing room variety

Output: the top 3 itineraries with Jaccard similarity below 0.6, three genuinely different trips rather than three near-duplicates. Given the same request and accepted set, the engine reproduces the same result: seeded, no Math.random().

Three Portals

Client, provider, operator.

Client
  • Fills one intake
  • Compares three itineraries
  • Books and pays
Provider
  • Works its net-rate inbox
  • Manages its own services and prices
  • Edits its own profile
Operator
  • Margin on every order
  • Provider status and inline catalog editing
  • Order cancellation
Price is partitioned. Providers only ever see net rates, clients only the all-in price. The split is enforced in one place and cannot leak between surfaces.
The LLM

Fenced, never in charge.

The model does two additive things and fails safe to deterministic defaults on any error. Every response is Zod-validated before it is trusted.

What it does
  • + Extract dietary and mobility constraints from free text
  • + Write provider-personalization notes
  • + Fail safe to deterministic defaults on any error
What it can never touch
  • × Validity of a day or a trip
  • × Pricing and the markup
  • × Availability and scoring weights

Deterministic code owns everything that decides feasibility or money. The model advises at the edges; it never gets the final say.

By Design

What was left out, on purpose.

A portfolio build proves a pattern end to end. What is missing is a scope decision, not an oversight.

Out of scope by choice
  • × Real payment, gateways, and PCI
  • × A live provider channel: chat or WhatsApp
  • × Rich media per experience and stay
  • × Transport as a bookable service
  • × Authentication and scale
What production would add
  • + Real catalog sourcing
  • + Provider comms as a mobile app
  • + A media pipeline
  • + Lodging and transport as contracted suppliers
  • + Auth, persistence, and concurrency revisited
